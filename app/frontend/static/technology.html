Technical Foundation & Pipeline

🤖

Custom Object Detection

We trained our own deep learning models (Mask R-CNN variants) to detect advertising boards with pixel-level accuracy.
Why not use ready models? Because every stadium and league is different. Our network was tailored for real, messy sports video.

🎯

Tracking & Seamless Integration

The “magic” isn’t just detection. We use fast, GPU-based tracking (RAFT & custom logic) to follow each board over time, enabling smooth transitions and perspective correction even with fast camera moves and scene cuts.

🛡️

Safety: Multi-Model Filtering

To prevent errors, we run parallel models for humans, balls, and other moving objects (COCO / pretrained Mask R-CNN).
Billboards are only replaced when we’re 100% sure it’s safe — this is not a toy, it’s production-ready AI.

⚡

Everything Runs on GPU

The entire pipeline — from detection to mask blending and video rendering — runs on CUDA and GPU acceleration (PyTorch, TensorRT).
No CPU bottlenecks, no off-the-shelf “scripts.”
